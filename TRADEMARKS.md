- **Forks and other deployments must use their own name and logo.** Do not call your deployment x402git, and do not present it in a way that suggests it is x402git.com or run by its operator.
- **Nominative references are fine.** "Built on x402git", "a fork of x402git" or "compatible with x402git" are welcome, as long as they are accurate and your own name is the prominent one.
- Remove the x402git icon and replace the operator's legal pages (`public/terms.md`, `public/buyer-terms.md`, `public/abuse.md`) before you run a public deployment.

Questions about using the name: `abuse@x402git.com`.
